# Minimum due, explained — Accrevise reel

**On-screen hook (beat 1):** Minimum due, *explained.* · ₹50,000 · 3.5% a month · 5% minimum

**Spoken hook to record:** "Rohan paid his credit card bill on time, every month. He'll still be paying it 23 years from now."

## Caption
Paying the "minimum due" on time feels responsible. It's the most expensive way to clear a card.

On a ₹50,000 bill at 3.5% a month + GST, paying only the 5% minimum takes ~23 years and costs ~₹2.2 lakh.
Pay a flat ₹2,500 instead and you're done in 39 months. Pay the full bill and interest is ₹0.

## Assumptions (also on the last frame)
₹50,000 balance, 3.5% a month interest + 18% GST on interest (≈ 4.13% a month), minimum due 5% of the balance with a ₹200 floor, no new spends, interest only on the unpaid balance. Rates, minimums and floors differ by card.

## Voiceover script (Kokoro af_heart, speed 1.1, one line per beat)
1. Minimum due on your credit card, explained.
2. Rohan buys a new phone for fifty thousand rupees. When the bill comes, he pays only the minimum. Two thousand five hundred.
3. The minimum due is about five percent of your bill. Pay it, and there's no late fee. But the rest keeps costing you three and a half percent a month, plus G S T.
4. So here's Rohan's bill. Fifty thousand owed. Three and a half percent a month. Five percent minimum.
5. One year later, he has paid about twenty-eight thousand rupees. And he still owes almost forty-four thousand. Only about six thousand actually cut the debt. The rest was interest.
6. Now meet a second Rohan. He starts with the same two thousand five hundred. But he never lets it shrink.
7. So, how long until he's debt-free?
8. Thirty-nine months. That's it. And the first Rohan, paying the minimum? He's still paying.
9. Because the minimum due is not the minimum cost.
10. As his balance falls, his minimum falls too, all the way down to two hundred rupees. So the debt drags on for about twenty-three years, and costs him over one lakh rupees extra.
11. To be fair, in one tight month, paying the minimum beats missing it. No late fee. No hit to your credit score. Just not every month.
12. The rule is simple. The more you pay each month, the less you pay overall. Pay the full bill, and the interest is zero.
13. Minimum due. Maximum interest.
