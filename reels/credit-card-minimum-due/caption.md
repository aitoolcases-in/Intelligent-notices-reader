# Minimum due, explained — Accrevise reel

**On-screen hook (beat 1):** Minimum due, *explained.* · ₹50,000 · 3.5% a month · 5% minimum

**Spoken hook to record:** "Rohan paid his credit card bill on time, every month. He'll still be paying it 23 years from now."

## Caption
Paying the "minimum due" on time feels responsible. It's the most expensive way to clear a card.

On a ₹50,000 bill at 3.5% a month + GST, paying only the 5% minimum takes ~23 years and costs ~₹2.2 lakh.
Pay a flat ₹2,500 instead and you're done in 39 months. Pay the full bill and interest is ₹0.

## Assumptions (also on the last frame)
₹50,000 balance, 3.5% a month interest + 18% GST on interest (≈ 4.13% a month), minimum due 5% of the balance with a ₹200 floor, no new spends, interest only on the unpaid balance. Rates, minimums and floors differ by card.
