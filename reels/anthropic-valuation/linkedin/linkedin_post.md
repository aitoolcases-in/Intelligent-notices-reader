Anthropic, the company behind Claude, went from a $380B valuation in February to $965B in May, and listed on the stock market this week.

Most of the noise is about the number. The number isn't the story.

A valuation is a bet on the future. A chai stall making ₹2 lakh a year can sell for ₹50 lakh if the buyer believes it becomes a chain.

What's real is who's paying: Anthropic's revenue grew fast, mostly from businesses paying for AI to do actual work.

For a small business, that's the signal. Not to buy any stock, but to ask one question:

What can AI do for my business this week?
