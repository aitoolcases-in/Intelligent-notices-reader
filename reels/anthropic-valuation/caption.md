## Caption
Anthropic's valuation? Most people get it wrong. Here's what it really means 👇

Anthropic (the company behind Claude) went from a $380B valuation in Feb 2026 to $965B in May, and listed on the stock market this November.

What is a valuation? Picture a chai stall making ₹2 lakh a year. A buyer offers ₹50 lakh, 25× its profit, because he's betting it becomes a chain. That's a valuation: a bet on the future, not a price tag on today.

Investors are making that bet because Anthropic's revenue grew fast, mostly from businesses paying for Claude.

The real story isn't the number. It's who's paying. Businesses everywhere now pay for AI work, and your competitors are learning it too.

To be fair: bets can be wrong (remember the dot-com bubble). This is not investment advice.

So skip the hype and ask one question: what can AI do for my business this week?

Save this and share it with someone who keeps asking "what's the big deal with AI?" 💡

- Nov 2026: IPO range set at $70–78 (CNBC, 10 Nov 2026); reported pricing at $83 on 18 Nov; trading on Nasdaq from 19 Nov. The reel only says "listed on the stock market".
- Revenue run-rate: ~$14B (Feb 2026) → over $47B (May 2026), per company statements as reported; mostly from business customers.
- Chai stall numbers are illustrative.
- Not investment advice.
