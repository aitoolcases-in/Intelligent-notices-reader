THE PROBLEM

Orders don’t arrive clean

Priya runs a sweet shop. About 40 orders a day come in like this:

THE REAL COST

Retyping, every evening

600 hrs

40 orders × 3 minutes = 2 hours a night. Over 300 working days, that’s 600 hours a year, plus the odd 2 kg billed as 2 boxes.
