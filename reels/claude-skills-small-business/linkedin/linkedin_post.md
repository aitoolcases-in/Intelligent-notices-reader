## Long version (original)

The slow part of small-business billing usually isn't the billing.

Picture Priya. She runs a sweet shop, and about 40 orders a day reach her like this:
"2kg KK – Sharma ji"
"same as last week pls"
…plus a counter book written in pen.

Every evening she reads each one and retypes it into her billing app. At ~3 minutes an order, that's 2 hours a night, roughly 600 hours a year, and the occasional 2 kg billed as 2 boxes.

Tally, Zoho Books and QuickBooks are excellent at invoices, GST and the books. None of them can read "KK, same as last week". That reading step is where a Claude Skill helps.

A Skill is a saved set of instructions and files that Claude uses whenever a job comes up. Priya teaches it three things, once:
1. Her price list and nicknames ("KK" = kaju katli)
2. Her regular customers and their GST numbers
3. Three real orders, each with the correct bill

Each evening she drops in the day's WhatsApp screenshots and a photo of the counter book. Claude returns a clean sheet to import, and flags what it isn't sure about ("Rao: 2 kg or 2 boxes?") instead of guessing.

To build one:
→ Settings → Capabilities → Skills: switch on "skill-creator"
→ In a chat: "Help me make a Skill for my shop's orders"
→ Upload the Skill it makes and test it on yesterday's orders

What it doesn't do: replace the billing app, read WhatsApp on its own, or remove the need to check every line before billing.

The software keeps the books. Claude does the reading.

If you run or advise a small business: which step in your week is still pure retyping?
