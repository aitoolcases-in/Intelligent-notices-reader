**Description**
You don't need to be a techie to automate your work. You just need to ask better questions.

In this video, Aryan (a young shop owner who always forgets follow-ups) and Meera (his accountant, who emails invoices and reconciliations to 12 clients every month) set up a simple AI automation with Claude: no code, about 10 minutes.

What you need:
• A paid Claude plan
• The Claude desktop app
• Gmail (and Google Drive if you keep files there)

Steps:
1. Settings → Connectors → connect Gmail (and Google Drive)
2. Claude app → Scheduled → New task → Create with Claude
3. Ask better questions: WHO (exact email addresses), WHAT (follow up / reply / update), WHEN (e.g., every weekday at 9 AM), HOW (tone, and whether to wait for your approval)
4. In the Gmail connector's tool settings, choose "Needs approval" (Claude drafts, you send) or "Always allow" (Claude sends on its own; on Team/Enterprise plans your admin may need to enable this)

Aryan's prompt:
"Every weekday at 9 AM, check my Gmail for unanswered mails from [client emails]. Draft a short, polite reply or follow-up for each. Wait for my approval before sending."

Meera's prompt:
"On the 5th of every month, for each client folder in Drive › Clients, find the latest invoice and reconciliation and email the Drive links to that client with a short note. CC me. Wait for my approval."

Good to know: the Gmail connector shares Google Drive links; it doesn't attach files. Check folder sharing so each client can open their documents.

Stay in control: watch the first few runs, and never automate a mail you wouldn't want sent by mistake.

Screens in this video are simplified illustrations; menu names may change. Check Claude's help centre for your plan.

## Facts used (checked Nov 2026)
- Scheduled tasks: Claude app → Scheduled → New task → "Create with Claude"; also /schedule. Paid plans.
- Gmail connector can send, reply and forward (added Aug 2026); approval is requested by default.
- Per-tool settings: Always allow / Needs approval / Blocked. On Team/Enterprise, admins control whether "Always allow" is permitted for write tools.
- Gmail connector reportedly cannot attach files, so Drive links are shared instead.
