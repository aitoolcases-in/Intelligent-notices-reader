1. Does it repeat every week?
2. Is it reading, writing or sorting?
3. Could a new helper learn it in 5 minutes?

## Voiceover script (Kokoro af_heart, speed 0.95, one line per beat)
1. AI is not for my business. Let's test that.
2. Gupta ji has run his hardware shop for twenty-five years. He's sure AI isn't for him.
3. He's half right. The question isn't, should I use AI? It's, which of my jobs fit it?
4. So he lists his week. Six jobs.
5. Then he asks three questions of each job. Does it repeat every week? Is it reading, writing or sorting? Could a new helper learn it in five minutes?
6. Talking to customers? Keep it human. Checking stock by eye? Hands-on. Supplier deals? His judgment.
7. So, what's left?
8. Two jobs pass all three. Answering, is it in stock, on WhatsApp. And sending payment reminders.
9. And GST billing? His billing software already does it. Don't add AI where software works.
10. Those two jobs take about eight hours a week. That's where AI fits. Nowhere else.
11. To be fair, if nothing passes the test, you don't need AI yet. That's a good answer too.
12. Your turn. List your week. Ask the three questions. Start with one job that passes.
13. Don't ask if AI is for you. Ask which job is.
