## Caption
Made a profit. Bank is empty? Here's where the cash went 👇

Priya's sweet shop had a record Diwali: ₹6 lakh in sales, ₹1.2 lakh profit on paper.
On 1 November, her bank had ₹10,000, with ₹2.1 lakh in bills due.

Where did the cash go?
💸 ₹3 lakh sold on credit ("will pay after Diwali")
📦 ₹1.5 lakh of extra stock on the shelf

Profit is what you earn on paper. Cash is what you can spend today.

3 cash habits:
1️⃣ 50% advance on bulk and office orders
2️⃣ A due date and a weekly reminder on every credit sale
3️⃣ Buy stock in batches, not all at once

Bonus: every Monday, a 4-week cash calendar (money in vs money out). AI can build it from your bank statement in minutes.

Profit is an opinion. Cash is a fact.
