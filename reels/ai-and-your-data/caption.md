## Caption
"AI will take my data." The worry is fair: whatever you paste into an AI app is sent to that company. So decide what goes in.

3 rules to use AI without risking your customers:

1️⃣ Turn training off
Most AI apps let you stop your chats being used to train them. On personal plans it's often ON by default.
• Claude: Settings → Privacy → "Help improve Claude"
• ChatGPT: Settings → Data controls → "Improve the model for everyone"
On a business plan? Read its data terms.

2️⃣ Use the traffic light
🔴 Never paste: Aadhaar, PAN, passwords, card numbers, OTPs
🟡 Hide details first: "Sharma ji" → "Customer A", remove phone and GST numbers
🟢 Safe: price lists, product details, templates, your own drafts

3️⃣ Check every answer. AI can be wrong.

Don't avoid AI. Decide what it sees.
