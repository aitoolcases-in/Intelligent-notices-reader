How many days is your money stuck in your business? Most owners don't know. Here's how to see it on one screen 👇

Ronnie's sales are ₹30L a month… but cash always feels short.

1️⃣ Export 4 reports from Tally / Zoho / Busy / Excel: sales, purchases, debtors (ageing) and stock

📕 Debtor days 42 → customers take 6 weeks to pay

Collect in 30 days instead of 42 → ₹12 lakh of his own cash freed up.

4️⃣ Refresh every month and watch the trend.

## The numbers (illustrative, consistent)
- Sales ₹30L/month = ₹1L/day; receivables ₹42L → 42 debtor days
- Cost of goods ₹24L/month = ₹0.8L/day; stock ₹28L → 35 inventory days; payables ₹22.4L → 28 creditor days
- Cash conversion cycle = 35 + 42 − 28 = 49 days
- Debtor days 42 → 30 frees 12 × ₹1L = ₹12L
- Healthy ranges vary by industry. Not financial advice. Only use "Comment DASHBOARD" if a prompt will actually be sent.
